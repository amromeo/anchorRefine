# Results after the Stage 1 anchor check

[Download the 80-row table](ablation-postcheck-summary.csv)

Each row summarizes one development sample size, pathology stratum, fitting method, anchor panel and randomized probability integral transform (PIT) seed. Each dataset has equal weight. All estimates use native refineR limits. `baseline` is unchanged refineR, `K24` is the full candidate, and `peak`, `start` and `region` use only the named part of the K = 24 rule. Each arm is compared with unchanged refineR on the same datasets, panel and PIT seed.

The `guidance` panel contains the 40 anchors available during fitting. The `diagnostic` panel contains 40 fresh, independent anchors. The release decision uses the guidance panel and primary seed. For development dataset `j`, the primary and alternate guidance PIT seeds are `62000000 + 10*j + 4` and `62000000 + 10*j + 5`. The fresh-panel seeds use offsets 6 and 7. A statistical decline requires p < 0.01.

- `checked_mean_capped_loss` averages over every planned dataset. A released interval contributes the mean of its two absolute endpoint z errors, each capped at 5. A decline, fit failure, check failure or missing finite interval contributes 5. `paired_delta` is the mean within-dataset difference from checked refineR. Negative values favor the arm. `kept_mean_capped_loss` averages only over released intervals with two finite limits.
- `decline_rate` divides statistical declines by evaluable checks (keep or decline). The table gives counts for planned datasets, valid fits, scored fits, fit and check failures, structural incompatibilities, evaluable checks, declines, returned intervals and nonreturns.
- Each fit’s pre-check interval determines its own error group using the larger of its two absolute endpoint z errors, `E`. `bad_gt1` and `severe_gt5` measure the share of fits with E > 1 and E > 5 caught by a statistical decline. `good_lt025` measures the share of fits with E < 0.25 lost to a statistical decline. The `all_valid` and `evaluable` columns give the counts of scorable fits in each group before and after requiring an evaluable check. Those counts coincide in every row because every fitted model had an evaluable check. The `declined` column is the numerator for both populations. The `at_boundary` columns count exact equalities to the thresholds. A blank rate indicates an empty denominator.
- The 95% intervals use 2,000 paired percentile bootstrap resamples of the factorial cells in each sample-size/pathology stratum (NumPy PCG64 seed 37000000). Replicates stay within their cell. The rate columns count resamples with an empty denominator. Interval endpoints are blank for fewer than two informative cells or no finite bootstrap rates.

The table uses the archived outputs from [development ablation run 45293475762](https://github.com/amromeo/anchorRefine/actions/runs/45293475762). For unchanged refineR and full K = 24, the decline and capture counts and rates on both panels and both seeds reproduce the original development report. The primary guidance checked losses, coverage and matched K = 24 differences and their confidence intervals also reproduce that report.
